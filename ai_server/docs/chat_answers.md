## Urgent safety advice
*i have chest pain*

```
Stop exercising now.
1) Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, sudden weakness or numbness, slurred speech, or severe swelling: call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.
2) Other new pain, dizziness or a fall: sit down, rest, and contact your doctor before exercising again.
```

## Afraid of falling  (`fear_of_falling`)
*I am scared I will fall*

```
Being afraid of falling is common.
1) Keep one hand on a sturdy chair or kitchen counter, wear non-slip shoes, clear the floor and keep your phone within reach.
2) If you live alone, do the standing exercises when someone is at home, and skip them on days you feel unsteady.
3) If you fall, do not get up quickly: rest, check for pain, roll onto your side, crawl to a sturdy chair and push up slowly.
4) If you are hurt or cannot get up, call for help or 999 and keep warm.
5) Tell your doctor about any fall or near-fall; they can check your balance and medicines.
```

## Checking with your doctor first  (`doctor_clearance`)
*why can't I get a plan, it says check with my doctor first*

```
Some conditions ask in your health profile whether they are under control (for example asthma or blood pressure).
1) Under control means your symptoms are stable with your usual treatment and your doctor has not told you to avoid exercise.
2) If you answered No for a condition that needs it, the app does not make a new exercise plan until your doctor agrees, because exercise could make it worse.
3) When your doctor says it is OK, open Profile, change that answer to Yes and save; then you can get a plan again.
```

## Exercising in hot weather  (`hot_weather`)
*it is very hot, can I exercise?*

```
On hot days exercise indoors in a cool, airy room, or outdoors only before 11 am or after 4 pm.

2) Older adults and people with heart disease or diabetes are more at risk in the heat, so take extra care.
```

```
3) Your plan already stays within the safety limits from your health profile.
```

## Following more than one plan  (`multiple_plans`)
*can I follow two plans at the same time*

```
You can follow more than one plan: up to 3 at a time if your risk level is Low, 2 if it is Medium, and 1 if it is High.
1) Two plans with the same goal that train the same joints clash, so the app asks you to replace one instead of adding the second; a shoulder plan and a hand plan can be followed together.

3) To add a plan, open AI-assisted planning and choose Retrieve physical treatment.
4) To stop one, open it on the Physical Treatment page and tap Stop this plan; your progress so far is kept.
```
