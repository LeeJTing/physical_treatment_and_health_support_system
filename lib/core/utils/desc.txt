Small helpers with no dependencies on the layers: FormValidators and validator_utils (form rules), id_utils (newId), session_policy (SessionPolicy, FR100_034), login_attempts (LoginAttempts, FR100_031, kept in the app preferences), code_attempts (CodeAttempts, FR100_016, wrong sign-up and reset codes, kept in the app preferences), date_utils, file_utils, body_regions (the body map regions). Any layer may use them.
